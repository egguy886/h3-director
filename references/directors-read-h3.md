# Director's Read for H3

Use this internal planning record before writing any narrative or performer-led H3 prompt. The record is for reasoning and production handoff; compile its meaning into visible or audible evidence instead of exposing its labels to H3.

## Classify the lane

Use the narrative lane when a person, creature, or performer pursues, resists, chooses, conceals, reacts, relates, or changes. Dialogue is not required.

Use the non-narrative lane for an environment study, packshot, material test, functional demonstration, motion transfer, abstract effect, or process visualization without character agency. Record only:

- `utility intent`: what the viewer must clearly see;
- `non-narrative refusal`: drama or psychology that must not be invented.

## Ten-field narrative record

Complete every field with a specific answer:

1. `dramatic function`: introduce, deepen, test, reveal, decide, turn, or pay off.
2. `turn`: the single readable before-to-after change.
3. `POV`: whose experience controls information and audience distance.
4. `power shift`: who controls the beat at the start and end, and what changes it.
5. `objective`: what the focal subject tries to obtain, protect, avoid, prove, or conceal.
6. `obstacle/tactic`: what blocks the objective and the playable action used against it.
7. `subtext/contradiction`: the gap between words and bodily truth.
8. `visible suppressed behavior`: one small impulse visibly contained, redirected, or leaked.
9. `non-transferable detail`: one exact story-bound object, costume fact, sound, place fact, ritual, or behavior.
10. `stock solution refused`: one generic genre shortcut rejected and the concrete replacement.

Do not use generic mood words as answers. When inventing a detail with user permission, mark it internally as an authored choice rather than source evidence.

## Compile abstractions into carriers

| Internal decision | H3-visible or audible carrier |
|---|---|
| POV | camera position, eyeline, withheld/revealed information, sound perspective |
| Power | height, frame share, distance, who advances, who yields |
| Objective | task, approach, retreat, reach, concealment, repeated attempt |
| Obstacle/tactic | visible interference followed by one response |
| Subtext | words contradicting grip, gaze, step, breath, or silence |
| Suppressed behavior | one timed micro-gesture held long enough to read |
| Turn | clear initial state, decisive action, changed endpoint |
| Non-transferable detail | exact prop, costume damage, sound, place, or ritual |
| Refused stock solution | a simpler replacement behavior or shot relationship |

At minimum, the final prompt must visibly or audibly carry the turn, the suppressed behavior, and the non-transferable detail.

## H3-specific discipline

H3 receives observable instructions, not an essay about intention. Do not write “she feels conflicted” if the shot can show her agreeing while tightening her grip. Do not ask for several emotional transitions in one 15-second module. The Director's Read may be rich; the compiled clip should be simple and legible.
