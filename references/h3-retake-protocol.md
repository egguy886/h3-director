# H3 Take Review and Retake Protocol

## Review evidence

Inspect the complete clip when possible, not only a thumbnail. Record duration, dimensions, orientation, frame rate, audio-track presence, and sampled frames around every planned cut and fragile action.

Never claim to have watched or heard media that the current tools cannot inspect. Distinguish observed evidence from user reports.

## Review dimensions

- story turn and first-three-second hook;
- identity, body, costume, form, and character count;
- spatial geography, axis, screen direction, and environment continuity;
- weight, support, force, contact, transformation, and consequence;
- camera motivation, stability, framing, and cut logic;
- performance behavior, eyeline, mouth visibility, lip sync, and dialogue timing;
- motivated light, exposure, fire/weather behavior, and color continuity;
- ambience, action sound, breath, dialogue clarity, music, and looping artifacts;
- final hook and usefulness of the observed endpoint.

## Verdicts

- `KEEP`: narrative and technical result is suitable for the cut.
- `POST-FIX`: core performance is usable; editing, crop, stabilization, grade, cleanup, or sound can solve the issue.
- `REROLL`: shot contract and assets are sound, but one generation variable failed.
- `REWRITE`: the action chain, spatial design, reference authority, timing, or prompt architecture caused the failure.

## One-variable repair

For `REROLL`, choose one primary lever:

- camera movement;
- action complexity or timing;
- body support/contact wording;
- reference role or removal;
- lighting/exposure;
- dialogue length/delivery;
- sound density;
- cut timing;
- endpoint.

Keep all accepted assets and other variables fixed. State the expected observable improvement.

Use `REWRITE` when the clip is being asked to show incompatible states, too many transformations, impossible geography, conflicting reference authority, or more beats than the duration can carry.

## Review output

```text
verdict:
score_or_readiness:
observed_strengths:
blocking_failures:
continuity_failures:
physical_failures:
camera_and_light_failures:
audio_failures:
observed_endpoint:
next_action:
single_changed_variable:
expected_improvement:
```
